Frequently asked questions

How many cameras does a home or shop need?

Usually 2–4 cameras for a home (gate, yard, entrance) and 4–6 for a small shop (entrance, till, sales floor, storeroom). We'll confirm the exact number after an assessment.

Which is better: an IP camera or an analogue one?

IP cameras give a sharper picture, take power and signal over a single cable (PoE) and are easy to expand. Analogue (AHD/TVI) is cheaper. For a new system we usually recommend IP.

Can I watch the cameras from my phone?

Yes. Once the recorder is online, the phone app shows live video and recordings from anywhere. We'll install and set up the app for you.

How many days of recordings are kept?

It depends on the disk size and the number of cameras. For example, four 2 MP cameras and a 1 TB disk give about 2 weeks. When the disk is full, the oldest recordings are overwritten automatically.

I forgot my recorder's password. What should I do?

Don't throw the device away — the password can be recovered or reset to factory settings. In most cases your old recordings are kept.

Can the camera see at night?

Yes. Cameras with infrared give a black-and-white picture up to 20–30 metres at night. There are also models that show colour even at night.
