Internet & Wi-Fi

Why is my Wi-Fi slow?

Common causes: the router sits in a corner, thick walls, too many connected devices or an outdated router. Often just moving the router and reconfiguring it helps.

Wi-Fi doesn't reach every room. What can I do?

Instead of a repeater, a mesh system or an extra wired access point is better — the connection doesn't drop as you move from room to room.

What is MikroTik and do I need it?

It's a professional router: two internet providers, a separate guest Wi-Fi, speed limits, VPN between branches. Great for offices, shops and hotels; not essential for an ordinary flat.

Websites and Telegram bots

How long does a website take?

A landing page (one-page site) takes 5–10 days; a company website with several sections takes 2–4 weeks. Timing also depends on how quickly the texts and photos are ready.

What can a Telegram bot do?

It takes orders and enquiries, shows your product catalogue, accepts payments, sends notifications to customers and keeps a customer list — all automatically.

Are the domain and hosting paid separately?

Yes, the domain (e.g. yourname.uz) and hosting are paid separately, once a year. I'll help you choose and connect them.

AI and automation

How is an AI bot different from a regular bot?

A regular bot only works with buttons. An AI bot understands questions written in the customer's own words and answers in Uzbek or Russian, based on your prices and services.

Won't the AI give customers wrong answers?

The bot is set up to answer only from your information and hands anything it doesn't know to a staff member. We test it together before launch.

Is there a monthly fee?

Yes, there is a small monthly fee for the AI service — it depends on the number of messages. I'll estimate it in advance.

What can be automated?

Anything you repeat every day: logging orders in a spreadsheet, preparing reports, reminders to customers, forwarding website enquiries to Telegram.

Video editing

How long does editing take?

Reels or a short ad take 1–2 days; wedding and event videos take 1–2 weeks. Rush jobs are possible too — just agree in advance.

What should I prepare for editing?

All the raw footage, your logo and music (if any), and an example video you like — that way the result will be exactly what you expect.

What is colour grading?

It's professional colour work on video: consistent colour across shots, natural skin tones and a beautiful mood. Even phone footage looks far more expensive.
